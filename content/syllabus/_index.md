### General Course Structure and Expectations

In this class, we link each of the conceptual topics listed below with its implementation in a mathematical model. For each topic, we will discuss readings and then play with the corresponding model. The models will be increasingly elaborate variations on one basic population model, not stand-alone, separate models. Between the reading assignments and the computer labs (group assignments), you should learn the concepts and gain some quantitative skills in modeling and coding. No previous coding or modeling experience is required.

There is also a final project for this course. You will get time in class towards the end of the course to work on the projects.

#### Attendance Policy

Two days can be missed with no impact on your grade (no need to submit make-up work), though I recommend that students attempt any missed class activities on their own time because subsequent class activities or discussions may rely on that knowledge.

If you miss additional days, you will need to submit make-up work. Class activities can be made-up on the student’s time within a week by doing the activity and submitting the code/data for instructor evaluation. If a student anticipates missing more than 6 class days (i.e. 3 weeks of classes), you must talk to me beforehand.

### AI policy

We acknowledge that everyone has different experience levels with coding and math, and AI is helpful for clarifying lines of code and troubleshooting. If you are less experienced with coding, we encourage you to use AI. If you use AI on the lab assignments, make a note on the assignment and let us know when you've used it- we don't care if you use it, but we want feedback on when it was most helpful and which labs are unclear or especially hard. This is the first time this version of the course is taught, so we would appreciate any feedback!

That being said, we hope you will at least try the assignments without it at first. As with anything, the more effort/time you put into the course, the more you'll get out of it.
